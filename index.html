About

I'm a data engineer who builds the pipelines, SQL datasets, and dashboards teams rely on to run their work.

At McCoy Federal Credit Union, I build daily data feeds into HubSpot, maintain ETL pipelines in SQL Server, and create Power BI dashboards for call center performance and member attrition. When reports run slow or a process depends on someone pulling data by hand, that's the work I like most.

I got into data engineering by necessity. When I ran my own logistics company, I taught myself Azure Data Factory to move our database to the cloud. Before that, I built Tableau dashboards and staffing forecasts at Verizon.

I'm based in Orlando and looking for my next data engineering role, and I'm open to relocating or working remotely.
